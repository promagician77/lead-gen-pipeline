AI Lead Gen Pipeline — n8n Architecture

For Engin's Upwork post, by James. MVP with n8n, Apify, OpenAI, and Gmail.

End-to-end n8n workflow

Each row is one n8n node. The whole pipeline runs automatically once you enter criteria.

📝
1. Criteria input"Almanya'da 10–100 çalışanı olan mobilya üreticileri" → parsed by OpenAI into structured search parameters: industry, location, employee range.
n8n Form
🔍
2. Company discoveryApify scrapes Google Maps and LinkedIn for matching companies. Returns name, website, location, size estimate, and description.
Apify
🌐
3. Website analysisApify crawls each company's website. OpenAI reads the page and scores fit (0-10) against your criteria. Below 6 → filtered out.
OpenAI
📧
4. Contact extractionHunter.io or Snov.io finds the best email contact. Verifies deliverability. No valid email → skipped, not guessed.
Hunter.io
✍️
5. Email personalizationYour fixed template + OpenAI adds 1-2 personalized sentences based on the company's website. You approve the template once, not per email.
OpenAI
📤
6. Email sendingGmail sends from your account with 60-90 second delays between emails. Daily limit respected. Each send logged in the CRM sheet.
Gmail
📥
7. Reply trackingGmail trigger watches for replies. OpenAI classifies: interested / not interested / question / out of office / bounce.
Gmail + AI
🔔
8. Opportunity alertIf classified as "interested" or "question" → sends you an instant Telegram/Slack/email notification with the reply and company context.
Telegram
📊
9. CRM / Google SheetEvery company tracked: name, website, contact, fit score, email sent, reply status, opportunity flag. One sheet = your pipeline.
Sheets

Decisions I'd want to settle with you

  • Approval before sending? I can add a step where you review and approve each batch before the emails go out, or it can run fully automatically. Most people start with approval and switch to auto once they trust the scoring.
  • Follow-up emails? The MVP does one email per company. Adding a 3-day follow-up for non-replies is a small addition if you want it.
  • Daily sending limit? Gmail allows ~500/day, but for cold outreach I'd recommend 30-50/day per account to protect deliverability. We can add more sending accounts later.
